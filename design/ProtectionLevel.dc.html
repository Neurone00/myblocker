Protection strength
Light
Blocks the big ad networks. Nothing ever breaks.
Balanced
Blocks ads and most trackers. Rarely needs a fix.
Strong
Blocks ads, trackers and phone telemetry. If an app misbehaves, allow it from Activity.

Stronger settings block more but may occasionally stop a feature in an app. When that happens, open Activity and tap Allow next to the app.
